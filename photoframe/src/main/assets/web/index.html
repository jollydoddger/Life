Only when we're home

Use each phone's fixed IP (set a DHCP reservation in your router) and turn off "private / random Wi-Fi address" for your home network on the phone.

How it shows

Screensaver: your photos come on whenever the TV has been idle for a few minutes, and any button takes you back. App: the frame stays on screen all the time and reopens itself after a restart and when you come home.

When off

Black uses local dimming, so the backlight is almost off. "Let TV sleep" lets the TV's own screen-off timer take over and tries to wake it again.
